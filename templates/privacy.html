Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and how we keep your financial data safe.

Last updated: October 2026

01Data We Collect

We collect only what Spendly needs to work:

  • Account details — your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense records — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Basic technical data — such as browser type and request logs, used to keep the service running.

We do not connect to your bank accounts or collect card or payment details.

02How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to provide the service to you — storing your expenses, showing category breakdowns and summaries, and letting you sign in securely. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone for marketing purposes.

03Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database and are accessible only to you when signed in. Passwords are never stored in plain text. We keep your data for as long as your account is active, and you may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

04Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address and browser details when a page loads. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers, and we do not share your expense data with third parties unless required by law.

05Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want to request access to, or deletion of, your data, contact us at privacy@spendly.example.
